## Contra summary

PACKSHIFT is an interactive packaging game and spatial compiler. It begins at the real 56 mm width of a carton, turns mandatory content into visible surface pressure, introduces a hydraulic press as a physical consequence, and ends by exhibiting the same object in a museum with its journey preserved as provenance. A professional mode reveals the deeper system underneath: a Blender-authored package, GLB runtime, pressure-by-face logic, dieline decomposition, redistribution and refold.
